About Nagapattinam

Nagapattinam is famous for its religious heritage, with major Hindu temples like the Kayarohanaswami Temple and Soundararajaperumal Temple, and as the base for pilgrimage sites like Velankanni, known for the Basilica of Our Lady of Good Health
