A paragraph is defined by the unity and coherence of its ideas, not a specific number of sentences or a rigid length. There is no standard "19 paragraph" structure in English writing. Instead, paragraphs are designed to develop a single main idea, and their length varies depending on the context, audience, and purpose.

A new paragraph should begin when you introduce a new idea, a new point in an argument, a change in topic, or a new speaker in dialogue.
